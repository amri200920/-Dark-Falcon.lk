## Strict Usage Rules
1. Never stretch or distort the falcon emblem.
2. Maintain aspect ratio at all times.
3. Use controlled electric blue accents; avoid blinding neon saturation.
4. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` with subtle glow effects instead of aggressive flashing.
